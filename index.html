How did this start?

"I have come to the conviction that if you cannot translate your thoughts into uneducated language, then your thoughts were confused"

Many can learn an advanced topic, few can explain it to someone else. This project began with me realizing that I can do neither very well. A year and a half ago, on the Christmas of 2025, my dad gave me a thin yellow book called "Perturbations of Banach Algebra". I've always liked math, learning new things, messing with numbers, seeing what happens, but this time I hit a wall. The math was so far beyond me I couldn't begin understand it. I got stuck on the preface. I hope to get better at learning at a deep level, and communicating something effectively.
